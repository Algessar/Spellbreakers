# Spellbreakers

## 1. Game Overview

**Spellbreakers** is a third-person action-adventure game built around a simplified, highly readable interpretation of historical longsword combat inspired by the **Lichtenauer tradition**.

The central combat concept is:

> **The player attacks by passing through guards.**

Guards are the meaningful combat states. An attack is a transition from one guard to another, and the transition itself is the attack.

The game is not intended to be a historically exhaustive fencing simulator. Historical swordsmanship provides the foundation for the combat language, while the game prioritizes:

- Readability
    
- Responsiveness
    
- Timing
    
- Positioning
    
- Physicality
    
- Fun
    

The broader game combines:

- Lichtenauer-inspired longsword combat
    
- Deliberate positioning, timing and spacing
    
- Zelda-like exploration
    
- Relatively simple environmental interaction and puzzles
    
- A bright-ish, grounded medieval setting
    
- Connected 3D environments
    
- Difficulty somewhere between classic Zelda and Dark Souls
    

The desired player experience is:

> **"I'm actually fighting this person with a sword."**


## World/Lore

The framing for the game is taken from a larger book and world-building project. It takes place
a few years after the events of the book, where the main character, Raine, is re-establishing the Watchers.
The Watchers were a neutral faction dedicated to the policing of magic-users. Magic is hazardous with cataclysmic
risks; the magical energies are drawn from the Void, the place in-between realities. The Void is also the place
where entities of darkness reside, i.e. demons.
The Watchers were eradicated hundreds of years ago, along with a significant chunk of magical knowledge. With that 
knowledge now being recovered Raine has taken it upon himself to re-establish this neutral faction and make an attempt
to police the use of magic.

Aiding in this is the sword in Raine's possession; Ancuil, one of the Spellbreakers - ancient artefacts with the power to deflect and 
dispel magic, and even destroy demons. It is unknown how many Spellbreakers exist, but at the time of and during the course of the game a total of 7 are located.
And each of them are inbedded into an Arch Demon - a creature having been possessed by a particularly strong demon. In ancient times
these demons turned out to be impossible to kill and were instead Sealed by the power of these Spellbreakers by sacrificing them and leaving 
them in place.

When it comes to magic in this game, the Core Magic (not explained in these docs) of the world will not be used; it is too complex and does not fit. Instead,
the Secondary magic system will be used: Spirits. These are entities drawn to skill and beauty; a counterpoint to demons. When executing
perfect combos, Spirits will gather and grant an immediate, temporary boon. These can be things like speed boosts or a low-absorb shield.
The boons granted are personality-influenced and can in the lore be varied (think Bard skills in DnD), but in this game limited to the four elements.
