# 23. World and Setting

The setting is a bright-ish medieval world with a grounded historical foundation, broadly inspired by the **13th–14th century**.

The world should feel recognizably medieval rather than generic fantasy.

Historical grounding provides the visual and physical foundation, while fantasy elements remain possible.

The atmosphere can be:

- beautiful
    
- bright
    
- dangerous
    
- ancient
    
- mysterious
    

without becoming relentlessly grimdark.
