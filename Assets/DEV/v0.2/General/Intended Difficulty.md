# 24. Intended Difficulty

The intended difficulty sits approximately between:

**Classic Zelda ← Spellbreakers → Dark Souls**

The game should be challenging enough that combat mastery matters without requiring the extreme punishment associated with Souls-like design.

A player who learns:

- guards
    
- distance
    
- timing
    
- enemy behavior
    
- positioning
    

should become substantially better at the game.

Difficulty should primarily come from understanding and executing the combat system, rather than inflated enemy statistics.
