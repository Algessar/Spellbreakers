# 22. Exploration

Outside combat, Spellbreakers should have a strong **Zelda-like exploration structure**.

The player should navigate through:

- environmental observation
    
- traversal
    
- discovering paths
    
- interacting with locations
    
- finding secrets
    
- relatively simple environmental problems
    

The game should not become primarily a puzzle game.

The intended balance is approximately:

> **Zelda exploration + fast/simple God of War-style environmental interaction.**
