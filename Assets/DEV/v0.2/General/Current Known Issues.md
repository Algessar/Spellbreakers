# 30. Current Known Issues

These are implementation problems currently affecting combat feel and should not be mistaken for settled design decisions.

- In-stance locomotion is too slow.
    
- Target acquisition is unreliable.
    
- Target reacquisition can fail after manual un-targeting.
    
- Target selection can choose an inappropriate target when several are available.
    
- Stance activation is currently awkward and may need to become automatic/contextual.
    
- Upper-body / Look At IK behavior needs improvement.
    
- The current transition implementation may not yet expose the complete guard-to-guard transition space.
    
- The transition animation approach is still being revised.
    

These should be addressed through playtesting rather than by prematurely locking down a technical solution.
