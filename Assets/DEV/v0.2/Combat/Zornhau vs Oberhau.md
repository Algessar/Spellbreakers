# 7. Zornhau vs Oberhau

The distinction between Zornhau and Oberhau is currently understood primarily as a difference in **motion and intent**, rather than simply damage.

### Zornhau

A Zornhau should generally feel:

- direct
    
- fast
    
- snappy
    
- relatively short in its path
    
- immediately committed toward the target
    

It may therefore end in an upper guard when that produces a particularly fast and direct attack.

### Oberhau

An Oberhau should generally involve:

- a longer arc
    
- more wind-up
    
- greater travel
    
- a more fluid transition
    

An Oberhau may also serve as a setup for the following transition.

For example:

```
Guard
  ↓
Oberhau
  ↓
New Guard
  ↓
Next attack
```

The resulting guard can therefore influence the quality and character of the next attack.

This is a design consideration rather than a rigid mechanical rule.
