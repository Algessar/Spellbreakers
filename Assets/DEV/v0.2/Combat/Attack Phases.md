# 8. Attack Phases

Each transition can conceptually be divided into three phases.

## ToPass

The weapon is being chambered/prepared.

The fencer:

- moves toward the attack line
    
- establishes edge alignment
    
- commits to the transition
    
- creates a readable telegraph
    

This phase does not necessarily deal damage.

---

## Strike

The weapon passes through the active attack line.

This is the primary damage window.

The blade should have meaningful velocity and a clear trajectory.

~~The attack may pass through an authored guard/pass pose at maximum extension.~~
The attack will pass through a Transition Pose, but may also pass through Correction Poses.

---

## Settle

The attack completes and the character arrives at the destination guard.

This is the follow-through/recovery portion of the transition.

The resulting guard is now the player's new combat state.

A successful parry may interrupt this process and leave the attacker vulnerable.
