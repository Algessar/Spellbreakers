# Spirits - Combo System

When it comes to magic in this game, the Core Magic of the world will not be used; it is too complex and does not fit. Instead,
the Secondary magic system will be used: Spirits. These are entities drawn to skill and beauty; a counterpoint to demons. When executing
perfect combos, Spirits will gather and grant an immediate, temporary boon. These can be things like speed boosts or a low-absorb shield.
The boons granted are personality-influenced, and in the case of Raine they are expressed as elemental magic; wind and fire. In the game, these
will be quite standard game mechanics:

Wind:
- Extra parry
- Pushback
- Move increase

Fire:
- Direct damage
- Area effect
- Damage over Time (general, it's fire)

Consideration: Adding in all four elements (Fire, Wind, Earth, Water) into the Spirits progression system to give the player
more choices. The Spirits system is still under brain-storming phase, but the core idea is a skill-tree where each element can be upgraded
in different ways.

### Progression System Design Suggestion:

You choose a tree by either:
- Building specific shrines where you can pay your respects
  (Inspired by Japanese shrines?)
- Upgrading the Home Base with decorations themed toward an element

Both would mean showing respect to the Spirits/the beautiful things in the world. And it'd be a bit more fun that just a UI popup. That would
also by necessity need to exist though.

Visuals: Spirits are small, glowing motes, reminiscent of the Fairies in Zelda. But without the wings. Will-o-the-wisp kind of deal.
